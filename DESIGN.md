# Design plan (frontend-design pass 1)

Reviewed against generic AI defaults and against RLC Residences' royal blue (#212a89) + gold (#daa900). Build only after this plan.

## Subject, audience, job
CRF Amazing Group, led by Crystal Flores, International Marketing Associate Director (Robinsons Land Corporation International Marketing). Filipino homebuyers, OFWs, and international buyers. Primary job: show projects and get a Viber/WhatsApp/call/email inquiry.

## Tokens (provisional logo colors; swap when logo file arrives)
- Navy `#0B1C3A` — header, dark bands, wordmark "Amazing Group"
- Navy deep `#07101F` — footer, overlays
- Gold `#C6A24A` — CRF letters, rules, primary buttons on navy, links
- Gold light `#E8D7A2` — hover, thin highlights
- Ivory `#F5F2EA` — page background (cool stone, not AI cream #F4F1EA)
- Ink `#12161C` — body text

## Type
- Display: Fraunces (soft, human serif) for headlines, her name, the quote.
- Body/UI: Atkinson Hyperlegible.
- Sentence case. No tracked all-caps eyebrows, no single recolored headline word, no middle-dot meta.

## Layout
Left-aligned, centered measure (~72rem max), growing gutters. Hero is typographic: name, title, the phone number, and contact actions — all above the fold on a laptop. No stock skyline (the real logo already carries one). Handover Releases is a dated notice board, not a card grid. Project lists are a typographic directory with one still image each.

## What makes this not generic
The notice-board releases + a large reachable number; navy is deep and quiet; gold is reserved; copy is hers ("Commit, Raise and Forward"); logo slot is a typographic wordmark until the real mark arrives.
